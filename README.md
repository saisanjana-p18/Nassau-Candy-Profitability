# Nassau Candy Distributor

## Product Line Profitability & Margin Performance Analysis

An interactive data analytics dashboard developed to analyze the sales and profitability performance of Nassau Candy Distributor.

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales, costs, gross profit, profit margins, product performance, divisional performance, and regional performance.

The dashboard helps identify profitable products, high-sales lower-margin products, profit concentration, monthly trends, and margin volatility.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, cost, gross profit, and units sold.
- Compare performance across different divisions and regions.
- Identify the most profitable products.
- Analyze product profit margins.
- Identify high-sales products with lower profit margins.
- Study monthly sales and gross profit trends.
- Analyze profit concentration using Pareto analysis.
- Examine monthly profit-margin volatility.
- Provide business recommendations based on the analysis.

## Dataset

The project uses the Nassau Candy Distributor dataset containing information related to orders, products, sales, costs, units, divisions, regions, and gross profit.

## Key Insights

- Chocolate was identified as the strongest division based on gross profit and profit margin.
- Pacific generated the highest total regional gross profit.
- Interior showed strong regional profit-margin performance.
- Wonka Bar - Scrumdiddlyumptious was identified as a high gross-profit product.
- Kazookles showed a relatively low profit margin and may require pricing or cost-control review.

## Business Recommendations

1. Prioritize high-profit products and divisions.
2. Focus promotional activities on profitable products.
3. Review low-margin products for pricing and cost-control opportunities.
4. Use region-specific strategies based on profitability.
5. Evaluate products using sales, units, gross profit, and profit margin together.
